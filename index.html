Key TO-BE Gaps Identified Across All Departments

No Internet + No Lan To Factory Office

There is no functioning Network Infrastructure.

Relationship B/n Yo & ETTA

There is issue with identifying actual supplier for Yo, Sub ETTA partner issues.

No Output Weight Sensor

No sensor exists on the production output side. Raw coal received (net weight at weighbridge) cannot be reconciled against washed coal produced. Recovery ratio is unknown.

Standalone Weighbridge Software

Project Balance 2012 / MIS-V12-5 operates in isolation with no network interface. Weight and GRN data cannot flow automatically into Odoo.

WeChat Production Reporting

Chinese production partners send conveyor weight readings as photos via WeChat. No formal system integration, no audit trail, no recovery data.

All Document Flows Are Paper-Based

Slip pads, freight orders, store request slips, weight slips, gate passes — all manual paper documents. Risk of loss, no real-time visibility.

Lab Results via WhatsApp

Raw coal lab results (the basis for supplier payment) are communicated to Finance via WhatsApp. No formal digital handoff or system linkage.

Coal Store Accountability Gap

One person covering 5–6 roles with no formal job description. Stock managed by estimation. No actual physical count of inventory performed.

Standalone Peachtree at Site

Site accountant (Wende) uses Peachtree in isolation. All data transfer to head office Finance (Meron) is physical paper documents.

Customer Lab Result Dependency

YO Holdings must wait for customer's own lab test results before finalising both sales invoices and supplier payments. No contractual SLA on turnaround.

Lab Sampling Wait Time

Lab sampler must wait for all 10–15 trucks from the same supplier to dump before sampling can begin — creating unpredictable delays in the lab cycle.

Attendance System — 4 Locations

Fingerprint system only at head office. Sites (Borreda, Fwafwate, Denkaka) rely on manual timekeepers. No unified attendance system across all locations.

Training Records Not Tracked

HR provides training but has no system to track who has completed what training. Compliance risk given hazardous coal environment.

Fuel Loans to Suppliers

There is no method of tracking loaned fuel to third party suppliers.

Black Market Purchased Fuel Issue

Yo inflates the number of liters it actually purchased to meet the price difference between black market price and legal price .

Mismatch on sold washed coal vs Customer received

Yo pays its sold coal based on lab results of its customers, customer result might show a weight difference based on their lab results.

⑤ Gap Analysis

Standalone Weighbridge Software

Project Balance 2012 / MIS-V12-5 has no network interface. Driver info, weight data, and GRN records cannot flow automatically into Odoo. All data must be re-entered manually.

Lab-Based GRN Delay (Path A-Yes)

For suppliers with lab-based payment contracts, the GRN cannot be generated until the moisture lab result is available. This creates an unpredictable delay in the receipt confirmation and can hold up supplier payment processing.

Telegram as Official Order Channel

Marketing sends production orders and truck plate lists via Telegram group chat. This is informal, has no audit trail, and cannot be linked to Odoo sales orders or delivery orders automatically.

All Outbound Documents Are Paper-Based

Delivery notes, freight orders, weighbridge ticket copies, and gate passes are all filled out manually on paper pads. No digital record at point of issuance. Risk of loss, duplication, or error.

No Output Weight Sensor on Production Side

Inbound raw coal net weight is captured at the weighbridge. However there is no corresponding output weight measurement from production — making recovery ratio calculation impossible.

Trailer Plate Tracking

Trucks with trailers require both the truck plate and trailer plate to be recorded. This needs to be supported in the Odoo vehicle/fleet module or a custom field in delivery records.

Open Ticket Management

Tickets are left open between gross weight and tare weight measurements. In a paper-based system, open tickets can be lost, forgotten, or mixed up — especially on high-volume days with multiple trucks.

Gate Pass Correctly Applied

Gate pass is correctly used only for outbound coal (both washed and raw coal sales). Inbound raw coal deliveries correctly do not require a gate pass. This separation is well understood operationally.

Sampling Wait Time — Type A

The lab sampler must wait for all 10–15 trucks from the same supplier to arrive and dump before sampling can begin. This creates unpredictable delays before the lab cycle even starts, holding up supplier GRN generation and payment.

Results Shared via WhatsApp

Raw coal lab results — which directly determine supplier payment amounts — are communicated to the Plant Manager via WhatsApp. This is informal, has no audit trail, and cannot be automatically linked to Odoo purchase orders or vendor bills.

Manual Result Registration

All lab results (for all 3 test types) are recorded manually in a log book. There is no Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS). Results cannot be directly accessed by Finance, Procurement, or Management without physical review of the log.

No LIMS Integration

With Odoo, lab results for Type A (raw coal) should ideally trigger the GRN automatically and update the vendor bill with the correct moisture-adjusted weight or value. Currently this is a fully manual multi-step process across lab, WhatsApp, and Finance.

Type B & C Results Not Utilised Systematically

Produced coal and dispatch coal test results are filed manually for data reference but there is no system tracking them. These results could be valuable for quality trend analysis, customer dispute resolution, and process optimisation in Odoo.

Retest Policy Not Formalised

Retests are described as "rare — only when results seem significantly off." There is no defined threshold, no documented escalation process, and no record of when retests have been triggered. This should be formalised as a quality control rule in Odoo.

Slip Pad Never Used

A slip pad exists to formally record the transfer of raw coal from the coal store to the production team. It is never used in practice. This means there is no documented handover — no quantity, no timestamp, no accountability for what raw coal entered production.

No Output Weight Sensor

The entry conveyor sensor records raw coal input. However there is no output weight sensor after production. The coal store cannot verify how much washed coal it is receiving from the Chinese production team. Recovery ratio (raw coal in vs washed coal out) is completely unknown.

Entry Conveyor Weight via WeChat Photo

The entry conveyor sensor displays weight on an internal module inside the factory. This weight — which is the basis for paying the Chinese partners — is captured by taking a photo of the display and sending it via WeChat. No system record, no audit trail, no ERP linkage.

Chinese Partner Payment Has No Verified Data

Chinese partners are paid based on the conveyor sensor reading shared via WeChat photo. There is no independent verification of this reading, no export from the sensor, and no cross-check against an output weight. This is a significant financial control gap.

G2 Fine / Non-Fine Separation Not Formally Tracked

Fine G2 and non-fine G2 take different paths (dryer vs direct conveyor) but there is no formal measurement or record of how much fine vs non-fine coal is produced. This quality split should be tracked in Odoo for inventory and quality management.

G1 & Filter Press Waste Not Quantified

G1 (1st degree waste) and filter press coal (2nd degree waste) are discarded but their volumes are not formally measured or recorded. Quantifying waste is essential for recovery analysis and future process optimisation in Odoo.

CS-2: Slip Pad Never Used

The coal store tracks how much raw coal it sent to production only via WeChat photos of the conveyor display sent by the Chinese partners at end of day. This is entirely informal, unverifiable, and cannot be linked to any ERP record.

CS-1: Lab-Based GRN Delay

For suppliers with lab-based payment contracts, the coal is dumped but the GRN cannot be created until the lab result arrives. This delays formal inventory receipt, which in turn delays Finance from creating the supplier payable and processing payment.

No Standardised Item Codes (General Store)

The general equipment store uses item codes assigned by the storekeeper at receipt, but there is no standardised coding system. This makes it difficult to track item history, reorder levels, or cross-reference between stores at different locations in Odoo.

Multiple Locations — No Unified Inventory System

The coal store operates at 3 locations (Borreda, Fwafwate, Denkaka) and the general store at 3 locations (HQ, Borreda, Fwafwate). Each location manages its own physical inventory with no centralised visibility or inter-location transfer tracking.

Both outbound dispatch processes (washed coal and raw coal sales) correctly route through the weighbridge and require a gate pass before trucks depart. This control is functioning as intended.

⑤ Gap Analysis

Standalone Accounting Systems at Sites

Borreda site uses Peachtree (standalone) and HQ uses Meron for GL/CoA. These systems are not integrated. Inter-site transactions must be manually reconciled with no real-time visibility between locations.

Petty Cash — No Fixed Reconciliation Schedule

Site petty cash (ETB 200,000) has no fixed reconciliation schedule. This is a financial control risk. Odoo should enforce periodic reconciliation with defined approval thresholds.

Coal Payment Requires Manual Excel + Lab Results

Raw coal supplier payment depends on an Excel sheet of receiving details plus lab results shared via WhatsApp. This manual chain — with no system linkage between lab, weighbridge, and Finance — creates risk of payment errors and delays.

Rental Truck Payments — Freight Order Dependency

Finance processes rental truck payments only once the first copy of the freight order is received from Transport. If freight orders are delayed, lost, or incomplete, payments are held up with no digital tracking of outstanding freight obligations.

Driver Allowances — Manual Requests

Driver allowance requests from Transport to Finance are handled manually with no digital workflow. In Odoo, these should be configured as expense requests with supervisor approval before Finance processing.

No Integrated Budget vs Actual Monitoring

Budgeting and forecasting are done manually without an integrated system linking budget targets to actual expenditure in real time. Odoo's budget management module should provide live variance tracking.

Document Requirements Are Well Defined

The Finance department has clearly defined the document requirements for both credit-based and cash-based vendor payments. This level of definition makes the process straightforward to configure in Odoo's vendor bill and payment workflows.

Cost Build-Up for Imports Not Systemised

Import landed cost calculations (purchase + shipping + duties + tariffs) are done manually. Odoo's landed costs feature should automate this and ensure accurate inventory valuation for imported items.
